# Skirt cloth motion

`ClothMotion` in ViolaCore supplies three damped springs using frame elapsed time. Lateral motion responds to filtered attachment acceleration, support shear, lower-leg swing and toe-hook stretching. Vertical motion and a slower fold wave supply small hem lift and follow-through. The integrator caps elapsed time at 0.1 seconds and subdivides it to at most 1/120 second; attachment velocity, acceleration, targets and final positions are bounded. Reduced Motion returns a stationary cloth frame and clears stored momentum.

`ClothRig` in ViolaDesktop consumes an existing loaded CGImage and SpriteDefinition. Optional polygon/cutout masks and mirroring are applied to a runtime texture without changing source PNGs. Up to 32 horizontal rows each contain two affine triangles. Every adjacent triangle shares its boundary coordinates, and a 0.4-point triangle-mask stroke absorbs subpixel rasterization edges. The source texture's upper twenty percent, including the waist and compressed seated pleats, remains pinned. Only the lower free cloth shifts laterally, lifts slightly and changes hem width by less than one percent. There is no whole-skirt animation rotation. Affine patches use the same renderer path for native Core Animation and CALayer PNG exports.

LayerRenderer creates optional rigs only for manifest sprite IDs `viola_skirt` and `friend_skirt`. Viola's attachment follows the existing seated body translation and breath/laugh bounce; her cloth also responds to both legs and toe-hook reach/straightening. The friend's skirt follows the existing palm-fixed support affine map, then adds cloth inertia at its upper edge. The existing torso, face, hands, shoes, desk and input rigs retain their current behavior. AnimationFrame carries the Reduced Motion flag explicitly, because reduced main-body amplitudes alone do not disable inertia.

Artwork must provide the original skirt silhouette and seated compressed folds. This rig preserves texture content and source garment length; it does not synthesize missing drape, alter the seated thigh exposure or shorten a skirt. The uppermost polygon/image row is treated as the garment attachment. Where the skirt is extracted from a combined body sprite, that same garment region must be excluded from the retained body to avoid duplicate artwork. Place each skirt sprite at its intended layer order relative to hips, thighs and the friend's face.

Validation: `swift build --target ViolaDesktop` completed successfully after the module and renderer integration. No tests, UI acceptance run, installation, packaging or code-signing actions were performed for this module. Cloth render quality and overlap with final skirt assets require inspection once those assets and polygons are present. `LayerRenderer.clothDiagnostics` exposes patch count, waist/hem rows and the current bounded spring outputs for that inspection.

## 0.2.14 renderer correction

Transparent costume art exposed diagonal mask seams in the triangle implementation. ClothRig now uses at most 32 full-width rectangular strips with shared affine boundaries and no diagonal masks. The pinned top and spring drive remain; width is fixed so adjoining strips stay continuous.
